Catalyst dalam satu napas

ID

Catalyst mengubah keyakinanmu tentang dunia menjadi strategi trading yang berjalan sendiri. Kamu menulis dengan bahasa biasa. AI agent meriset, memilih instrumen (token, perps, saham, prediction market), menyusun workflow, memantau sinyal, lalu mengeksekusi dalam batas yang kamu setujui. Dana tetap di wallet non-custodial milikmu.

Catalyst is the intent layer for all of finance. Tujuan konkret

  • Intent → eksekusi, end-to-end. “People will simply state an intent, and we have our agents to figure out everything end to end.”
  • Semua pasar di satu tempat. Spot token, perps, saham tertokenisasi, prediction market, “with more onchain and offchain assets coming soon”.
  • Hedging untuk semua orang. “A farmer shouldn't need a hedge fund to be able to hedge.”
  • Edukasi risiko yang personal. “We're building a lot of user education tools that can explain the safety and risks.”
  • Retail trading for everyone. Research preview dulu, lalu dibuka per batch dari waitlist, lalu tersedia luas.
  • Aman tapi seru. “Can we make investing safely just as fun as sports gambling? I think we can.”

Masalah yang diselesaikan

Investasi selalu dimulai dari ide, tapi mengubah ide menjadi trade butuh keahlian: memilih instrumen, kapan memakai leverage, membuat trade yang efisien biaya, dan menentukan pemicunya. Orang biasa akhirnya membayar manajer atau sekadar membeli dan berharap. Trading institusi sudah algoritmik, sementara keuangan pribadi masih manual.

Contoh Sequoia: kopimu makin mahal → kamu membaca soal kemarau di Brasil → yakin harga terus naik → Catalyst mengubah firasat itu menjadi strategi yang terstruktur dan sudah diuji.
how many times have you said “i knew that was gonna happen” and didn't trade on it. never again.Jake Paul, investor via Anti Fund

FAQ siap jawab

Ada token / airdrop?
REPLY · EN

No official token has been announced. Any “Catalyst” token you see on Solana is not affiliated, so please don't buy it. Follow @CatalystLabsX for official news.

Jangan pernah menjanjikan airdrop. Laporkan link token palsu ke tim.

Kapan saya dapat akses?
REPLY · EN

Access is rolling out in batches from the waitlist. Make sure you signed up at catalyst.app with the email you check, and keep an eye on your inbox, including spam.

Apakah berbayar?
REPLY · EN

Catalyst is free during the research preview.

Aman? Dana dipegang Catalyst?
REPLY · EN

Catalyst is non-custodial. Your funds sit in your own wallet (powered by Privy), and Catalyst can't access them. Agents only act within the budget and conditions you approve, and you can pause or change a strategy anytime. Market risk still applies, so start small.

Bisa trading apa saja?
REPLY · EN

Spot tokens, perpetuals, tokenized stocks, and prediction markets, with more onchain and offchain assets coming soon. You can combine them in one workflow.

Deposit dan withdraw?
REPLY · EN

Deposit with Apple Pay, credit card, or crypto (USDC, SOL, ETH, BNB). Withdrawals are in stablecoins for now, and fiat withdrawals are coming soon.

Catalyst Cash itu apa?
REPLY · EN

Catalyst Cash is the internal capital routing system. Deposit once in any supported currency, and Catalyst routes your capital to whichever venue a trade needs. No manual bridging or network switching.

Bedanya dengan bot trading / ChatGPT?
REPLY · EN

It doesn't just chat. Catalyst turns your idea into a visual workflow (signals → conditions → trades), monitors live data across markets, and executes within limits you approve. Every action is verifiable end-to-end.

Ini judi?
REPLY · EN

The team has been clear: “We're not here to build a casino.” The goal is safe, trustworthy finance, giving everyday people tools like hedging that used to need a hedge fund, plus education on risk.

Tersedia di negara saya?
REPLY · EN

Availability can differ by region and by market, since each venue has its own rules. The team will share details as access expands, so stay tuned to announcements.

Signals: BI announcement, USD/IDR, DXY, Polymarket odds on next Fed cut
Workflow I'd ask Catalyst for:

  ELSE → notify me only
Open question: how would the agent choose spot vs perp here?

Jalur ke Regional Moderator

  1. Konsisten di jam Asia. Aktif setiap hari saat tim AS tidur. Coverage zona waktu adalah alasan utama proyek AS butuh regional mod.

  3. Kuasai produknya begitu dapat akses. Mod yang bisa membantu troubleshooting deposit dan workflow jauh lebih berharga.
  4. Bawa nilai di luar Discord. Thread X yang akurat tentang Catalyst, dengan tag @CatalystLabsX.
  5. Ajukan diri saat waktunya tepat. Saat ada pengumuman program mod/ambassador, atau setelah kamu dikenal. Jangan spam DM founder.
